Early Years

Stories for Nursery and Reception

Robots, Robots Everywhere

By Sue Fliess · Illustrated by Bob Staake

A lively story full of robots helping with everyday tasks, inviting children to imagine what robots can do and how they might help us.

123 Do The Robot

By Michelle Robinson

A playful counting story that invites children to join in with robot moves, count along and explore numbers through movement and rhythm.

Dot

By Randi Zuckerberg

Dot is a tech-savvy girl who loves using her devices, but discovers that the world is full of adventures beyond the screen. A story about balancing screen time with outdoor play, creativity and real-world experiences.

Years 1–2

Stories for our younger learners

Chicken Clicking

By Jeanne Willis · Illustrated by Tony Ross

A chicken discovers online shopping and makes a new friend on the internet. A fun story that introduces online safety and the importance of being careful with strangers online.

Ava in Code Land

By Jess Hitchman

Ava discovers a world of coding and learns how instructions, patterns and problem-solving can help bring ideas to life. A playful introduction to computational thinking.

Years 3–4

Stories to explore and discuss

Troll Stinks!

By Jeanne Willis · Illustrated by Tony Ross

Two young rabbits discover how easy it is to use a phone to send messages, but learn that online actions can hurt others. A story about kindness, cyberbullying and thinking before you post.

#Goldilocks

By Jeanne Willis · Illustrated by Tony Ross

Goldilocks becomes an online video star, but her quest for more likes leads her to share increasingly daring posts. A funny story about online privacy, oversharing and thinking carefully before posting.

Rox's Secret Code

By Mara Lecocq

A story about discovering and using a secret code. It introduces children to the idea that messages can be represented in different ways and encourages pattern-spotting and logical thinking.

What’s an Algorithm?

By Kaitlyn Siu and Marcelo Badari

An introduction to algorithms: clear, step-by-step instructions that help us solve problems and complete tasks. It connects everyday sequences with the way computers follow instructions.

Hello Ruby: Adventures in Coding

By Linda Liukas

Join Ruby on an imaginative adventure that introduces the world of computers and coding. The story encourages curiosity, creativity, problem-solving and computational thinking.
